### ✨ Added

- First release. A strict, type-aware lint config for oxlint and `bun lint`, with
  `presets.base`, `presets.react`, and `presets.vitest`, `defineConfig`, and `FILE_GLOBS`.
- Requires `oxlint` 1.89 or newer. Tested with `bun lint` from Bun `1.4.4-canary.20261022.1`.
